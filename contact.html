How to order

Four easy steps, all on WhatsApp.

  1. Choose your lampTap Order on WhatsApp on the lamp you like.

  3. Choose how to payPay in full by M-Pesa, or book with a 50% deposit and pay the balance on delivery.
  4. Send it on WhatsAppYour message is already written. We reply to confirm the delivery day and time.
