We have two kinds of lights

Table lamps

We have designed these with your use in mind, so you will find them useful for ambient lighting by your bed, in a corner of your living room, next to a favourite chair, or in any other place that might need a soft, warm glow. Floor lamps

These reach and fit areas that a table lamp cannot, such as a corner of a main sitting room, bedroom, next to a sofa or by a bookshelf. They give a house a more welcoming atmosphere, even though the reason for this is not necessarily that the light is required.
