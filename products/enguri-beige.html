The name comes from “enkukuri” and “ingri,” Maasai terms for a narrow-necked gourd that a household uses every day. It holds milk, water, or porridge, sometimes plain, sometimes hand-carved and decorated. Enguri is modelled after the simpler, everyday version of this gourd: reliable, understated, and always available when needed.
