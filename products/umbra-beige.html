Learn more about this lamp

The Umbra is a mid-century inspired floor lamp, designed with safety as well as style in mind. A tall, slim shade sends a column of warm light up and down your wall.

Safety and stability

  • Safe wiring: PVC-insulated, sheathed 3-core flexible cable (0.75mm²) with an earth wire, to protect you in case of an electrical fault.
  • Secure cable: Cable grips hold the cable firmly where it enters the metal and again at the top.
  • Stable: The weight is well distributed and the base is heavy. In our tests the lamp only topples at around an 18° tilt, about three times the 6° test angle for indoor portable luminaires in KS IEC 60598-2-4.
  • Convenient: It comes with a CE- and ENEC-marked footswitch (2A, 250V).
