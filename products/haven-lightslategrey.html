Haven draws its inspiration from the kiondo — the handwoven basket carried by generations of Kenyan women, home from the fields with food, harvests, and the fruits of their labour. More than a basket, it became a symbol of care and resilience.

Haven is also inspired by the crown. Not as a symbol of power, but as an honour given to those who enrich their families and communities.

Together, the two ideas take one form: a vessel that carries what matters, standing tall.

Haven is handcrafted from solid mahogany and wrapped in premium cotton linen. Quiet strength. Thoughtful craftsmanship. Beauty that lasts.
