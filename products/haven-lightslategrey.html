The base of Haven was inspired by the traditional handwoven basket carried by generations of Kenyan women. Used for decades to carry the food, harvests, and hard work that feed families as they return home from the fields, it naturally speaks of resilience and care.

Above it, the shade pays tribute to the crown — honouring those who lift up, guide, and enrich their families and communities.
